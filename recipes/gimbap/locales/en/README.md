# Gimbap

![Gimbap cover image](images/cover.webp)

A classic home-style Korean seaweed rice roll filled with egg, carrot, spinach, pickled radish, and ham.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Rice Dish, Korean

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice — 400 g
- Gimbap seaweed sheets — 2
- Eggs — 2
- Carrot — 1/2 (about 80 g)
- Spinach — 80 g
- Pickled yellow radish (danmuji) — 2 long strips
- Ham — 80 g
- Sesame oil — 2 tsp
- Salt — 1/2 tsp
- Toasted sesame seeds — 1 tsp
- Cooking oil — 2 tsp

## Directions

1. Season 400 g warm cooked rice with 1 tsp sesame oil, 1/4 tsp salt, and 1 tsp toasted sesame seeds. Mix gently without crushing the grains, then let it cool until just warm.
2. Beat the 2 eggs with a small pinch of salt. Heat 1 tsp cooking oil in a pan, cook the eggs into a thick omelet, and cut it into long strips.
3. Julienne the carrot and stir-fry it with 1/2 tsp cooking oil and a small pinch of salt for 2 to 3 minutes. In the same pan, heat the remaining 1/2 tsp cooking oil and stir-fry the spinach with a small pinch of salt for about 1 minute, until wilted.
4. Cut the ham into thin strips and warm it in the pan for about 1 minute, then pat the danmuji dry.
5. Place 1 seaweed sheet rough-side up on a bamboo mat. Spread half the rice in a thin, even layer, leaving about 2 cm uncovered along the top edge.
6. Lay half the egg, carrot, spinach, danmuji, and ham across the middle of the rice in long rows. Roll tightly from the bottom so the filling is enclosed, then rest the roll seam-side down. Repeat for the second roll.
7. Brush the outside of both rolls with the remaining 1 tsp sesame oil, slice into bite-size pieces, and serve.
