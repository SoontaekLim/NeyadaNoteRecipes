# Baek Jong-won–Style White Kimchi (Baek-kimchi)

![Baek Jong-won–Style White Kimchi (Baek-kimchi) cover photo](images/cover.webp)

A small-batch, non-spicy napa cabbage kimchi adapted from SBS's Delicious Rendezvous. Clear Korean lemon-lime soda and salted shrimp give the brine a refreshing savory flavor.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Side Dish, Korean

## Ingredients

- Napa cabbage — 1/2 head (about 800g)
- Coarse salt (for brining) — 105g
- Water (for brining) — 1.5L
- Sweet rice flour — 6g
- Water (for kimchi brine) — 1L
- Salted fermented shrimp (saeujeot) — 40g
- Minced garlic — 40g
- Onion — 1/2
- Korean lemon-lime soda (soda cider) — 125ml
- Korean radish (mu) — 1/4 (about 250g)
- Scallions — about 40g
- Red chili pepper — 1

## Instructions

1. Cut the half head of napa cabbage lengthwise into two wedges. Dissolve 105g coarse salt in 1.5L water, submerge the cabbage, and brine for 2–3 hours, turning once. The thick white ribs should bend without snapping.

2. Rinse the salted cabbage 2–3 times under running water, then set it upside down in a colander to drain for about 30 minutes.

3. Whisk 6g sweet rice flour into 200ml taken from the 1L of kimchi-brine water. Cook over low heat, stirring for 2–3 minutes, to make a thin slurry. Let it cool completely.

4. Julienne the 1/4 radish, cut the scallions into 5cm lengths, and slice the red chili pepper thinly on the diagonal.

5. Blend the onion, 40g saeujeot, 40g minced garlic, and 125ml lemon-lime soda. Mix with the cooled rice slurry and the remaining 800ml brine water. Strain through a fine sieve if you prefer a clearer brine.

6. Gently mix the radish, scallions, and red chili. Tuck small portions between the leaves of the well-drained cabbage.

7. Pack the stuffed cabbage into a clean, lidded container. Pour over the brine from step 5 and gently press the cabbage below the surface.

8. Leave covered in a cool spot out of direct sunlight for 12–24 hours, then refrigerate. Let mature for another 1–2 days in the fridge; taste and cut into serving pieces.

**Source:** [SBS Delicious Rendezvous — Baek Jong-won's white kimchi](https://programs.sbs.co.kr/enter/2019taste/visualboard/62961?board_no=3625&cmd=view). This is a home-size adaptation at roughly one-quarter of the televised batch; some amounts are based on estimates in secondary coverage because exact on-air measures were not available.
