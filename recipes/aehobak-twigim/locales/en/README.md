# Crispy Korean Zucchini Fries (Sung Si Kyung Style)

Korean zucchini sticks seasoned with soy sauce and chicken stock, tossed in potato starch and fried until crisp. Served with a tangy, spicy sriracha mayonnaise dip.

- Servings: 2 servings
- Tags: Drinking Snack, Snack, Korean

## Ingredients

- Korean zucchini (or regular zucchini) — 1 (about 300g)
- Brewed soy sauce — 1 tbsp
- Concentrated liquid chicken stock — 1/2 tbsp
- Potato starch — 5 tbsp (3 tbsp + 2 tbsp)
- Ground black pepper — 1/4 tsp (1/8 tsp each for seasoning and dip)
- Neutral frying oil — 150ml
- Olive oil — 50ml
- Mayonnaise — 3 tbsp
- Sugar — 1/2 tbsp
- Lemon juice — 1 tbsp
- Minced garlic — 1/2 tbsp
- Salt — 1 pinch
- Sriracha chili sauce — 1 tbsp

## Steps

1. Wash and pat dry the zucchini, then trim both ends. Cut into sticks 6–8cm long and about 1.5cm thick, leaving the skin on.

2. Place the zucchini, 1 tbsp soy sauce and 1/2 tbsp liquid chicken stock in a food-safe bag and gently massage to coat. Use less stock if it is particularly salty.

3. Add 1/8 tsp pepper and 3 tbsp potato starch, close the bag with some air inside and shake. Add the remaining 2 tbsp starch and shake again. Tap off any clumps and fry promptly.

4. Heat 150ml neutral oil and 50ml olive oil in a deep-sided frying pan over medium heat to 150–160°C. Never fill the pan more than halfway. Add the zucchini one piece at a time with space between them and fry for 4–6 minutes, turning with tongs. If necessary, increase the temperature to 170°C until golden and crisp. Drain on a wire rack for 1–2 minutes. Fry in batches if needed.

5. Mix 3 tbsp mayonnaise, 1/2 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tbsp minced garlic, a pinch of salt, 1/8 tsp pepper and 1 tbsp sriracha in a small bowl. Plate the fries and serve immediately with the dip on the side.
