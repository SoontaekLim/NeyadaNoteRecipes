# Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry

![Tomato and Egg Stir-Fry cover image](images/cover.webp)

Inspired by Chef Lee Yeon-bok’s method, this Chinese-style tomato and egg stir-fry balances crisp-tender tomatoes with soft, fluffy eggs.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Side Dish, Chinese

## Ingredients

- Tomatoes — 2 (about 300g)
- Eggs — 5
- Liquid chicken stock seasoning — 1 spoonful
- Cooking oil — 2 spoonfuls
- Scallion — 1/4 stalk
- Water (to blanch and rinse tomatoes) — As needed

## Instructions

1. Remove the stems from the 2 tomatoes and score a shallow X on the bottom of each. Briefly blanch in boiling water until the skins start to loosen, then lift them out.
2. Beat the 5 eggs in a bowl. Add 1 spoonful of liquid chicken stock seasoning and mix well.
3. Rinse the blanched tomatoes in cold water and peel them. Cut into small pieces and mix them into the beaten eggs.
4. Finely chop 1/4 stalk of scallion and stir it into the tomato-and-egg mixture.
5. Heat 2 spoonfuls of cooking oil in a pan over medium heat, then pour in the egg mixture. Do not stir immediately. As the edges set, gently push them toward the center with a wooden spatula, then flip the larger pieces to cook the other side.
6. When the eggs are fully cooked but the tomatoes are only about 70–80% cooked and still retain some bite, remove from the heat and transfer to a plate.
