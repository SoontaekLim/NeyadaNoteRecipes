# Chicken Fricassée (French Creamy Chicken Stew)

A comforting French chicken-and-mushroom fricassée gently simmered in broth, cream, and thyme.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Dish

## Ingredients

- Boneless chicken thighs — 400g
- Button mushrooms — 200g
- Onion — 1 (about 150g)
- Butter — 30g
- All-purpose flour — 1 tbsp (about 10g)
- Chicken stock — 250ml
- Heavy cream — 100ml
- Lemon juice — 1 tsp
- Dried thyme — 1/2 tsp
- Salt — 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper — 1/4 tsp

## Instructions

1. Cut 400g boneless chicken thighs into 4cm pieces and pat dry. Thickly slice 200g mushrooms and finely chop 1 onion.

2. Melt 20g butter in a deep skillet over medium heat. Lightly brown the chicken for 4–5 minutes; it need not be fully cooked yet. Transfer to a plate.

3. Melt the remaining 10g butter in the same skillet. Sauté the onion and mushrooms for 5–6 minutes until softened and much of the mushroom moisture has evaporated.

4. Sprinkle over 1 tbsp flour and stir for 1 minute, then gradually pour in 250ml chicken stock while stirring to make a smooth sauce.

5. Return the chicken and resting juices to the pan, then add 1/2 tsp dried thyme. Cover and simmer gently for 12–15 minutes until the chicken reaches at least 74°C internally.

6. Stir in 100ml heavy cream and heat gently for another 3 minutes. Season with 1/2 tsp salt, 1/4 tsp black pepper, and 1 tsp lemon juice, then divide between two servings.
