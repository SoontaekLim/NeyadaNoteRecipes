# Curry Rice

![Curry Rice cover image](images/cover.webp)

A comforting home-style curry rice with pork, onion, and carrot, lightly scented with cumin and served over steamed rice.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Dish, Rice Dish

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice — 2 bowls (about 400 g)
- Pork — 200 g
- Onion — 1
- Carrot — 1/2
- Curry roux — about 40 g
- Water — 350 ml
- Cooking oil — 1 tbsp, plus a little for frying eggs (optional)
- Ground cumin — 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper — 1/4 tsp
- Eggs — 2 (optional)

## Directions

1. Cut the onion into bite-size pieces. Using a generous amount of onion adds sweetness and body to the curry.

   ![Cooking step 1](images/step-1.webp)

2. Cut the carrot into bite-size pieces or slices about 1 cm thick.

   ![Cooking step 2](images/step-2.webp)

3. Cut the pork into bite-size pieces. Heat 1 tbsp cooking oil in a pot over medium heat and cook the pork for 3 to 4 minutes until the outside is no longer pink.

   ![Cooking step 3](images/step-3.webp)

4. Add 1/4 tsp black pepper and 1/2 tsp ground cumin and cook for about 30 seconds to release their aroma.

   ![Cooking step 4](images/step-4.webp)

5. Add the onion and cook for 4 to 5 minutes. Cover and cook for about 2 minutes so the onion softens and releases moisture, stirring once halfway through.

   ![Cooking step 5](images/step-5.webp)

6. When the onion has released enough moisture to make the pot look slightly saucy, add the carrot and cook for about 2 minutes.

   ![Cooking step 6](images/step-6.webp)

7. Cook for about 5 minutes more, then pour in 350 ml water. Once it boils, reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for about 10 minutes until the pork is fully cooked and the carrot is tender.

   ![Cooking step 7](images/step-7.webp)

8. Reduce the heat to low and add about 40 g curry roux a little at a time, stirring until fully dissolved. Cook for another 4 to 5 minutes, stirring to prevent scorching, until thickened.

   ![Cooking step 8](images/step-8.webp)

9. Divide the rice between bowls and spoon the curry over it. If desired, fry the 2 eggs and serve them on top.

   ![Cooking step 9](images/step-9.webp)
