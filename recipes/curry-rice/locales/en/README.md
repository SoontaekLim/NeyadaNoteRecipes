# Curry Rice

![Curry Rice cover image](images/cover.webp)

A comforting home-style curry rice with pork, onion, and carrot, lightly scented with cumin and served over steamed rice.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Dish, Rice Dish

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice — 2 bowls (about 400 g)
- Pork — 200 g
- Onion — 1
- Carrot — 1/2
- Curry roux — about 40 g
- Water — 350 ml
- Cooking oil — 1 tbsp, plus a little for frying eggs (optional)
- Ground cumin — 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper — 1/4 tsp
- Eggs — 2 (optional)

## Directions

1. Cut the onion into bite-size pieces and slice the carrot about 1 cm thick. Cut the pork into bite-size pieces.
2. Heat 1 tbsp cooking oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the pork and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the outside is no longer pink.
3. Add the ground cumin and black pepper and cook for about 30 seconds to release their aroma.
4. Add the onion and cook for 4 to 5 minutes. Cover for about 2 minutes so the onion softens and releases moisture, then add the carrot and cook for another 2 minutes.
5. Pour in 350 ml water. Once it comes to a boil, reduce the heat to medium-low and simmer for about 10 minutes, until the carrot is tender and the pork is fully cooked.
6. Reduce the heat to low and add the curry roux a little at a time, stirring until fully dissolved. Cook for another 4 to 5 minutes, stirring to prevent scorching, until thickened.
7. If using the eggs, heat a little cooking oil in a pan and fry them. Divide the rice between bowls, spoon the curry over it, and top with the fried eggs.
