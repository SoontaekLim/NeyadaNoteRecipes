# Tuna Kimchi Stew

![Tuna Kimchi Stew cover image](images/cover.webp)

A simple kimchi stew made with canned tuna and well-fermented kimchi, with tofu as an optional addition.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Soup/Stew, Korean

## Ingredients

- Well-fermented kimchi — 250g
- Canned tuna — 1 can (135g)
- Tofu (optional) — 1/2 block
- Onion — 1/4
- Green onion — 1/2 stalk
- Water — 500ml
- Korean red pepper flakes (gochugaru) — 1 tbsp
- Korean soup soy sauce (guk-ganjang) — 1 tbsp
- Minced garlic — 1/2 tbsp
- Cooking oil — 1/2 tbsp

## Instructions

1. Cut the kimchi into bite-size pieces and thinly slice the onion. Slice the green onion on a diagonal, and if using tofu, cut it into slices about 1.5cm thick.
2. Heat the cooking oil in a pot over medium heat and stir-fry the kimchi and onion for 2 to 3 minutes.
3. Add the water, gochugaru, soup soy sauce, and minced garlic. Once it comes to a boil, reduce to medium-low heat and simmer for about 10 minutes.
4. Lightly drain the canned tuna, add it to the pot, and stir once or twice so the tuna does not break up too much. Simmer for about 5 more minutes.
5. If using tofu, add it with the green onion and simmer for another 3 to 5 minutes. If skipping the tofu, add only the green onion, bring the stew back to a brief simmer, and finish.
