# Chuncheon-style Dakgalbi

![Chuncheon-style Dakgalbi cover image](images/cover.webp)

Chuncheon-style spicy stir-fried chicken made with chicken thigh, cabbage, sweet potato, rice cakes, and perilla leaves in a sweet-and-spicy gochujang sauce.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Dish, Korean

## Ingredients

- Boneless chicken thigh — 400 g
- Cabbage — 250 g
- Sweet potato — 150 g
- Onion — 1/2
- Green onion — 1/2 stalk
- Perilla leaves — 8
- Korean rice cakes (tteokbokki tteok) — 120 g
- Cooking oil — 1 tbsp
- Water — 80 ml
- Gochujang (Korean red pepper paste) — 2 tbsp
- Korean red pepper flakes (gochugaru) — 1 1/2 tbsp
- Korean soy sauce (jin-ganjang) — 1 1/2 tbsp
- Sugar — 1 tbsp
- Cooking wine — 1 tbsp
- Minced garlic — 1 tbsp
- Curry powder — 1/2 tsp
- Sesame oil — 1 tsp
- Ground black pepper — a pinch

## Directions

1. Cut the chicken thigh into bite-size pieces. Cut the cabbage into 4 to 5 cm pieces and the sweet potato into 5 mm half-moons. Slice the onion thickly, slice the green onion on the diagonal, and cut the perilla leaves into 2 cm strips. If the rice cakes are firm, soak them in cold water for about 10 minutes and drain.
2. Mix 2 tbsp gochujang, 1 1/2 tbsp gochugaru, 1 1/2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp cooking wine, 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1/2 tsp curry powder, and a pinch of black pepper to make the sauce.
3. Coat the chicken with half of the sauce and let it stand for about 10 minutes.
4. Heat a wide pan over medium heat and add 1 tbsp cooking oil. Add the seasoned chicken and sweet potato and stir-fry for about 3 minutes. Add the cabbage, onion, remaining sauce, and 80 ml water, mix well, cover, and cook over medium heat for 5 minutes.
5. Remove the lid and continue stir-frying for 5 to 7 minutes, turning the ingredients so they do not stick. Cook until the chicken is fully cooked through and the sweet potato is tender.
6. Add the rice cakes and green onion and stir-fry for another 2 to 3 minutes until evenly coated. Continue stir-frying until the sauce lightly coats the ingredients.
7. Just before turning off the heat, add the perilla leaves and 1 tsp sesame oil. Toss for about 30 seconds and serve immediately.
