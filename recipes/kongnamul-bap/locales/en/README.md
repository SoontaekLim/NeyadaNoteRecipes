# Kongnamul Bap (Korean Soybean Sprout Rice)

A comforting Korean rice bowl steamed with soybean sprouts and served with scallion-soy dressing.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Korean, Rice

## Ingredients

- Short-grain rice — 180g
- Soybean sprouts — 220g
- Water — 200ml
- Scallion — 25g
- Soy sauce — 2 tbsp
- Toasted sesame oil — 1 tbsp
- Gochugaru — 1 tsp
- Sesame seeds — 1 tsp

## Instructions

1. Rinse 180g rice until the water runs mostly clear, soak 15 minutes and drain. Rinse 220g soybean sprouts.

2. Put the rice and 200ml water in a saucepan and spread sprouts evenly on top. Cover and bring to a simmer over medium heat, then reduce to low.

3. Cook covered on low for 12 minutes, turn off heat and rest covered for 10 minutes. If the rice is still hard, add a splash of water and cook a little longer.

4. Thinly slice 25g scallion and combine with 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil, 1 tsp gochugaru and 1 tsp sesame seeds.

5. Gently fluff the rice and sprouts together, divide into two bowls and add the soy dressing gradually to taste.
