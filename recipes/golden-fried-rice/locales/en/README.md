# Golden Egg Fried Rice

![Golden Egg Fried Rice cover image](images/cover.webp)

A fluffy, golden Chinese-style egg fried rice adapted for home cooking from Chef Lee Yeon-bok's golden fried rice technique.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Rice Bowl, Chinese

## Ingredients

- Microwave-ready cooked rice — 2 packs
- Eggs — 3–4
- Spam luncheon meat — about 50g
- Carrot — about 70g
- Scallion — about 40g
- Chicken stock seasoning — 2/3 spoonful
- Cooking oil — 2–3 spoonfuls
- Water (for blanching Spam) — As needed

## Instructions

1. Microwave the 2 packs of cooked rice for 30 seconds to make the grains easier to separate.
2. Beat 3–4 eggs in a bowl. Add the warmed rice and gently press and mix with a wooden spatula until the egg coats the grains evenly.
3. Finely dice the carrot, add it to the egg-coated rice, then mix in 2/3 spoonful of chicken stock seasoning.
4. Finely dice the Spam, blanch it briefly in boiling water, drain it and mix it into the rice. Finely chop the scallion and mix it in as well.
5. Heat 2–3 spoonfuls of cooking oil in a pan, add the prepared rice mixture and stir-fry.
6. Continue until the eggs are fully cooked, the rice is golden and the grains are fluffy and separate. Transfer to plates.
