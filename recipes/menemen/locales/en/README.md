# Menemen (Turkish Tomato and Egg Skillet)

A comforting Turkish breakfast skillet of tomatoes and green pepper gently cooked with softly set eggs.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Dish

## Ingredients

- Eggs — 4
- Tomatoes — 3 (about 350g)
- Green pepper (or pointed pepper) — 1 (about 100g)
- Onion (optional) — 1/2 (about 75g)
- Olive oil — 2 tbsp
- Sweet paprika powder — 1/2 tsp
- Salt — 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper — 1/4 tsp
- Bread or baguette — 2 slices (about 120g)

## Instructions

1. Dice 3 tomatoes and thinly slice 1 green pepper. If using onion, finely chop 1/2 onion. Crack 4 eggs into a bowl, keeping the yolks intact.

2. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the pepper and optional onion for 4–5 minutes until softened.

3. Add the tomatoes, 1/2 tsp paprika, 1/2 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper. Simmer over medium heat for 6–8 minutes until the tomatoes soften and their juices thicken slightly.

4. Reduce heat to low and add the 4 eggs among the tomatoes. Gently break the yolks with a spatula and stir only two or three times. Cook 2–4 minutes until softly set.

5. Turn off the heat once the eggs are cooked to your liking, and serve immediately from the skillet with 2 slices of bread.
