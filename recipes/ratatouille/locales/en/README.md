# French Ratatouille

A Provençal vegetable stew in which eggplant, zucchini, peppers, and tomatoes are gently simmered with olive oil and thyme.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Side Dish, Main Dish

## Ingredients

- Eggplant — 1 (about 250g)
- Zucchini — 1 (about 250g)
- Red bell pepper — 1 (about 180g)
- Tomatoes — 3 (about 350g)
- Onion — 1 (about 150g)
- Garlic cloves — 2
- Olive oil — 3 tbsp
- Dried thyme — 1/2 tsp
- Salt — 3/4 tsp
- Ground black pepper — 1/4 tsp
- Water — 50ml

## Instructions

1. Cut the eggplant, zucchini, red pepper, and 3 tomatoes into roughly 2cm pieces. Finely chop the onion and mince 2 garlic cloves.

2. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a wide pot over medium-high heat. Sauté the eggplant and zucchini for 5–6 minutes until the edges color, then transfer to a plate.

3. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil to the pot. Sauté the onion and red pepper for 4 minutes, then stir in the garlic and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.

4. Add the tomatoes, 50ml water, and 1/2 tsp dried thyme. Bring to a simmer, then cook over medium-low heat with the lid slightly ajar for 10 minutes.

5. Return the eggplant and zucchini and simmer gently for another 15 minutes. Season with 3/4 tsp salt and 1/4 tsp pepper and serve in 2 bowls.
