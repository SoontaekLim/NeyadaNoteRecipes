# Tuna Kimchi Fried Rice

![Tuna Kimchi Fried Rice cover image](images/cover.webp)

An easy Korean rice dish stir-fried with well-fermented kimchi, tuna, and egg, with optional roasted seaweed flakes on top.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Rice Dish, Korean

## Ingredients

- Cooked rice — 400 g
- Well-fermented napa cabbage kimchi — 200 g
- Canned tuna — 100 g, drained
- Eggs — 2
- Onion — 1/4 (about 50 g)
- Green onion — 1/2 stalk (about 30 g)
- Kimchi juice — 2 tbsp
- Soy sauce — 1 tbsp
- Sugar — 1 tsp
- Cooking oil — 1 tbsp
- Sesame oil — 1 tsp
- Roasted seaweed flakes — 2 tbsp (optional)

## Directions

1. Cut the kimchi into 1 to 2 cm pieces, finely chop the onion, and thinly slice the green onion. Drain the tuna well and lightly beat the 2 eggs.
2. Heat 1/2 tbsp cooking oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the eggs and stir in large curds until softly set, then transfer them to a plate.
3. Add the remaining 1/2 tbsp cooking oil to the same pan and stir-fry the green onion and onion for about 1 minute. Add the kimchi and 1 tsp sugar, then cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the kimchi softens.
4. Add 400 g cooked rice and 2 tbsp kimchi juice, breaking up the rice as you mix. Pour 1 tbsp soy sauce around the edge of the pan and stir-fry over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes to cook off excess moisture.
5. Add the drained tuna and cooked egg. Stir-fry gently for about 1 minute so the tuna stays in visible pieces.
6. Turn off the heat and mix in 1 tsp sesame oil. Serve in bowls and, if desired, finish with 2 tbsp roasted seaweed flakes.
