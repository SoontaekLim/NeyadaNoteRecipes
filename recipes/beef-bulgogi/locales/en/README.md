# Beef Bulgogi

![Beef Bulgogi cover image](images/cover.webp)

A classic Korean beef bulgogi made by marinating thinly sliced beef in a sweet-savory soy sauce mixture, then stir-frying it with onion, green onion, and carrot.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Dish, Korean

## Ingredients

- Thinly sliced beef for bulgogi — 300g
- Onion — 1/2
- Green onion — 1/2 stalk
- Carrot — 1/4
- Soy sauce — 3 tbsp
- Sugar — 1 1/2 tbsp
- Minced garlic — 1 tbsp
- Sesame oil — 1 tbsp
- Ground black pepper — a pinch
- Cooking oil — 1 tbsp
- Sesame seeds — 1 tsp

## Instructions

1. Slice the onion about 5 mm thick, cut the green onion on a diagonal, and julienne the carrot.
2. In a large bowl, mix the soy sauce, sugar, minced garlic, sesame oil, and black pepper until the sugar begins to dissolve.
3. Add the beef, onion, green onion, and carrot to the marinade, toss well to coat, and marinate for about 20 minutes.
4. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan over medium-high heat, then add the marinated beef and vegetables.
5. Stir-fry for 5 to 7 minutes, separating the beef as it cooks, until the meat is fully cooked and the excess marinade has reduced slightly.
6. Turn off the heat and finish with sesame seeds.
