# Cold Kimchi Noodles (Kimchi Mari Guksu)

![Cold kimchi noodles cover image](images/cover.webp)

Refreshing Korean cold noodles with a sweet-tangy kimchi broth, thin wheat noodles, cucumber, and boiled egg.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Noodles/Pasta, Korean

## Ingredients

- Thin wheat noodles (somyeon) — 200 g
- Well-fermented napa cabbage kimchi — 150 g
- Kimchi brine — 100 ml
- Cucumber — 1/3
- Hard-boiled egg — 1
- Cold water (for broth) — 450 ml
- Water (for boiling noodles) — 1.5 L
- Vinegar — 2 tbsp
- Sugar — 1–1.5 tbsp, plus 1/2 tsp for kimchi (optional)
- Korean soup soy sauce (guk-ganjang) — 1 tbsp (or 1/2 tbsp fish sauce)
- Salt — a pinch
- Toasted sesame oil — 1 tsp
- Sesame seeds — 1 tsp
- Shredded roasted seaweed (gim) — a little (optional)
- Ice cubes — 6–8

## Directions

1. In a large bowl, stir together 450 ml cold water, 100 ml kimchi brine, 2 tbsp vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, and 1 tbsp Korean soup soy sauce (or 1/2 tbsp fish sauce). Taste and add up to 1/2 tbsp more sugar if needed, depending on the kimchi's tartness. Season with a pinch of salt and refrigerate until very cold.

   ![Cooking step 1](images/step-1.webp)

2. Cut 150 g kimchi into bite-size pieces and toss with 1 tsp toasted sesame oil and 1/2 tsp sesame seeds. If the kimchi is very sour, optionally add 1/2 tsp sugar. Julienne 1/3 cucumber and halve the hard-boiled egg.

   ![Cooking step 2](images/step-2.webp)

3. Bring 1.5 L water to a boil and cook 200 g somyeon according to the package, usually 3–4 minutes. Immediately rinse and gently rub the noodles under cold water 2–3 times to remove excess starch, then drain thoroughly.

   ![Cooking step 3](images/step-3.webp)

4. Divide the noodles between two bowls. Top with seasoned kimchi, julienned cucumber, and the egg halves. Pour in the chilled broth, add 6–8 ice cubes, and finish with the remaining 1/2 tsp sesame seeds and optional shredded roasted seaweed. Serve immediately.

   ![Cooking step 4](images/step-4.webp)
