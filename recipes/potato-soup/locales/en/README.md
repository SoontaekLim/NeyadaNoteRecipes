# Creamy Potato Soup

![Creamy Potato Soup cover image](images/cover.webp)

A simple, creamy potato soup made by gently cooking potatoes and onion, then blending them smooth with milk. Finish with a few drops of truffle oil if desired.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Soup/Stew, Western

## Ingredients

- Potatoes — 300g
- Onion — 1/2 (about 100g)
- Butter — 15g
- Water — 250ml
- Milk — 250ml
- Salt — 1/2 tsp
- Black pepper — A pinch
- Truffle oil (optional) — 1/2 tsp

## Instructions

1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into roughly 2cm pieces. Thinly slice the onion.
2. Melt the butter in a pot. Add the onion and cook over medium-low heat for 3–4 minutes until translucent.
3. Add the potatoes and 250ml water and bring to a boil. Reduce to low heat, cover and cook for 12–15 minutes, until the potatoes mash easily with a spoon.
4. Turn off the heat and let the mixture cool slightly, then blend the potatoes and onion until completely smooth using an immersion blender or countertop blender.
5. Return to low heat and stir in 250ml milk until warmed through. Season with 1/2 tsp salt and a pinch of black pepper. If the soup is too thick, add a little more water to adjust the consistency.
6. Serve as is, or finish each serving with a few drops of truffle oil for extra aroma.
