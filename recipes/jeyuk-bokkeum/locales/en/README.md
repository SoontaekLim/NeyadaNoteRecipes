# Jeyuk Bokkeum (Spicy Pork Stir-Fry)

![Jeyuk Bokkeum (Spicy Pork Stir-Fry) cover image](images/cover.webp)

A classic Korean spicy-sweet pork stir-fry made with a gochujang sauce, onion, and green onion.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Main Dish, Korean

## Ingredients

- Pork shoulder — 300 g
- Onion — 1/2
- Green onion — 1/2 stalk
- Cooking oil — 1 tbsp
- Gochujang (Korean red pepper paste) — 2 tbsp
- Korean red pepper flakes (gochugaru) — 1 tbsp
- Soy sauce — 1 tbsp
- Sugar — 1 tbsp
- Minced garlic — 1 tbsp
- Sesame oil — 1 tbsp
- Toasted sesame seeds — 1 tsp

## Directions

1. Cut the pork into bite-size pieces, slice the onion into thick strips, and cut the green onion on the diagonal.
2. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar, minced garlic, and sesame oil to make the sauce.
3. Add the sauce to the pork and toss until the pork is evenly coated.
4. Heat the cooking oil in a pan over medium-high heat. Add the seasoned pork and stir-fry for 4 to 5 minutes.
5. When the pork is almost cooked, add the onion and green onion. Stir-fry for another 2 to 3 minutes until the pork is fully cooked.
6. Turn off the heat and finish with the toasted sesame seeds.
