# Shakshuka

A North African and Middle Eastern-style skillet of eggs gently poached in spiced tomato and pepper sauce.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: North African, Brunch

## Ingredients

- Eggs — 4
- Canned tomatoes — 400g
- Red bell pepper — 1 (about 150g)
- Onion — 1/2 (about 80g)
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Olive oil — 1 tbsp
- Ground cumin — 1 tsp
- Sweet paprika — 1 tsp
- Salt — 1/2 tsp
- Bread — 2 slices

## Instructions

1. Dice onion and bell pepper into 1cm pieces and mince garlic. Have 4 eggs ready.

2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a skillet and sauté onion and bell pepper over medium heat for 6 minutes until softened.

3. Add garlic, 1 tsp cumin and 1 tsp paprika and cook for 30 seconds. Add 400g tomatoes and 1/2 tsp salt and simmer 8 minutes.

4. Make four wells in the sauce and crack an egg into each. Cover and cook on low for 5–8 minutes until the whites are set.

5. Remove from heat when the eggs are cooked to your preference and serve with 2 slices of bread. Cook longer if you prefer fully set yolks.
