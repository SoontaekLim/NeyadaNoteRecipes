# Pasta alla Norma

A Sicilian pasta with tender browned eggplant, tomato sauce, basil and a little grated cheese.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Italian, Pasta

## Ingredients

- Pasta — 180g
- Eggplant — 1 (about 300g)
- Canned tomatoes — 400g
- Garlic — 2 cloves
- Olive oil — 3 tbsp
- Fresh basil — 8g
- Ricotta salata or Parmesan — 30g
- Salt — 1 tsp (plus extra for pasta water)

## Instructions

1. Cut eggplant into 2cm cubes, thinly slice garlic, rinse basil and grate the cheese.

2. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a wide skillet and sauté eggplant over medium-high heat for 8–10 minutes until golden; set aside.

3. Add remaining 1 tbsp oil and garlic to the skillet for 30 seconds. Add 400g tomatoes and 1 tsp salt; simmer for 10 minutes.

4. Cook 180g pasta in salted boiling water for 1 minute less than package directions. Reserve 100ml pasta water and drain.

5. Add eggplant and pasta to the sauce and toss for 1–2 minutes, loosening with reserved pasta water as needed. Turn off heat and finish with 8g basil and 30g cheese.
