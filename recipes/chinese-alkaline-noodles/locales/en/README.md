# Homemade Chinese Alkaline Noodles

![Homemade Chinese Alkaline Noodles cover image](images/cover.webp)

Fresh, springy Chinese-style wheat noodles made with bread flour and food-grade baking soda. Ideal for jjamppong, ramen, or stir-fried noodles.

- Servings: 2
- Category: Noodles/Pasta, Chinese

## Ingredients

- Bread flour — 200g
- Water (for dough) — 85ml
- Salt — 2g
- Food-grade baking soda — 2g
- Cornstarch (for dusting) — 10g
- Water (for boiling) — 1.5L

## Instructions

1. Dissolve 2g salt and 2g food-grade baking soda completely in 85ml water. Use baking soda, not baking powder.

2. Place 200g bread flour in a bowl. Gradually add the prepared water while stirring. Once the flour forms small crumbs, press them firmly together into a single dough ball.

3. Wrap the dough or place it in an airtight container to keep it from drying out, then rest at room temperature for 30 minutes. It is normal for the dough to feel stiff and rough at first.

4. Put the dough on a work surface and press and fold it repeatedly for about 5–7 minutes. Cover again and rest at room temperature for 20 minutes.

5. Roll the dough to 1.5–2mm thickness and dust lightly with 10g cornstarch. Fold loosely, cut into strands about 2mm wide, then separate the noodles so they do not stick.

6. Bring 1.5L water to a boil, loosen the noodles into the pot, and cook for 2–3 minutes. Adjust for noodle thickness, drain well, then add to your preferred soup or sauce.
