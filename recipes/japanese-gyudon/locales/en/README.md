# Gyudon (Japanese Beef Rice Bowl)

Thinly sliced beef and onions simmered in a lightly sweet soy-dashi sauce and spooned over rice.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Japanese, Rice Bowl

## Ingredients

- Thinly sliced beef — 250g
- Onion — 1 (about 150g)
- Cooked rice — 2 bowls (about 360g)
- Water — 150ml
- Soy sauce — 2 tbsp
- Mirin — 2 tbsp
- Sugar — 2 tsp
- Instant dashi powder — 1/2 tsp
- Ginger — 10g

## Instructions

1. Thinly slice onion into 5mm strips and slice 10g ginger. Separate any clumps of beef.

2. Combine 150ml water, 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tbsp mirin, 2 tsp sugar, 1/2 tsp dashi powder and ginger in a saucepan; bring to a simmer.

3. Add onion and simmer over medium heat for 5 minutes until translucent.

4. Add 250g beef a few slices at a time and simmer 4–6 minutes until fully cooked. Skim foam and add a splash of water if the sauce reduces too much.

5. Divide warm rice between two bowls, top with beef and onion, and spoon over the remaining sauce to taste.
