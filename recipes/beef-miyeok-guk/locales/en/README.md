# Beef Miyeok-guk

![Beef Miyeok-guk cover image](images/cover.webp)

A classic Korean seaweed soup made by sautéing beef brisket and miyeok in sesame oil, then simmering until deeply savory.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Soup/Stew, Korean

## Ingredients

- Dried miyeok (seaweed) — 10g
- Beef brisket (yangji) — 120g
- Sesame oil — 1 tbsp
- Korean soup soy sauce (guk-ganjang) — 1 1/2 tbsp
- Minced garlic — 1 tsp
- Water — 1L
- Salt — 1/4 tsp

## Instructions

1. Soak the dried miyeok in cold water for about 10 minutes. Rinse it several times, squeeze out excess water, and cut it into bite-size pieces.
2. Pat the beef brisket dry with a paper towel and cut it into bite-size pieces.
3. Add the sesame oil and beef to a pot and cook over medium heat for about 2 minutes, until the outside of the beef is no longer raw.
4. Add the soaked miyeok and soup soy sauce, then sauté for another 2 to 3 minutes so the seaweed and beef absorb the seasoning.
5. Pour in the water and bring to a boil over high heat. Reduce to medium-low and simmer for about 20 minutes.
6. Add the minced garlic and simmer for 5 more minutes. Season with the salt, then turn off the heat.
