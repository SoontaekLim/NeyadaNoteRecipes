# Gambas al Ajillo (Spanish Garlic Shrimp)

Classic Spanish tapas of tender shrimp gently cooked in garlic-chili olive oil and served with bread for dipping.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Tapas, Main Dish

## Ingredients

- Peeled, deveined shrimp — 300g
- Garlic cloves — 7
- Olive oil — 100ml
- Dried red chili — 1
- Salt — 1/3 tsp
- Fresh parsley — 5g
- Baguette — 150g

## Instructions

1. Pat 300g peeled shrimp very dry with paper towels. Thinly slice 7 garlic cloves, halve 1 dried red chili, finely chop 5g parsley, and slice 150g baguette.

2. Place 100ml olive oil, sliced garlic, and dried chili in a small skillet. Warm over low heat for 3–4 minutes until the garlic becomes pale golden; do not let it brown.

3. Increase heat to medium and add the shrimp in a single layer. Cook for about 1–2 minutes per side, turning once.

4. When the shrimp are pink and opaque all the way through, add 1/3 tsp salt and turn off the heat. Avoid overcooking.

5. Sprinkle over 5g chopped parsley and serve immediately with the warm garlic oil and 150g sliced baguette for dipping.
