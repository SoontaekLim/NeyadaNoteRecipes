# Chicken Fajitas

Spiced chicken and sautéed peppers tucked into warm tortillas, Tex-Mex style.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Tex-Mex, Chicken

## Ingredients

- Chicken breast — 300g
- Bell peppers — 2 (about 300g)
- Onion — 1 (about 150g)
- Flour tortillas — 4
- Olive oil — 2 tbsp
- Ground cumin — 1 tsp
- Paprika — 1 tsp
- Chili powder — 1/2 tsp
- Salt — 1/2 tsp
- Lime — 1

## Instructions

1. Cut chicken into 1cm strips, and slice peppers and onion lengthwise. Prepare raw chicken and vegetables on separate boards.

2. Toss chicken with 1 tbsp olive oil, cumin, paprika, chili powder and salt.

3. Cook chicken in a hot skillet over medium-high heat for 5–7 minutes until it reaches at least 74°C internally, then set aside.

4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp olive oil, peppers and onion to the skillet and cook 5–6 minutes. Return chicken and cook 1 minute more.

5. Warm four tortillas for 20 seconds per side in a dry pan, fill with chicken and peppers and squeeze lime over the top.
