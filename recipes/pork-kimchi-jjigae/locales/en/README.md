# Pork Kimchi Stew

![Pork Kimchi Stew cover image](images/cover.webp)

A classic kimchi stew made with pork and well-fermented kimchi.

- Servings: 2
- Categories: Soup/Stew, Korean

## Ingredients

- Well-fermented kimchi — 250 g
- Pork shoulder — 150 g
- Tofu — 1/2 block
- Green onion — 1/2 stalk
- Water — 500 ml
- Korean red pepper flakes (gochugaru) — 1 tbsp
- Korean soup soy sauce — 1 tbsp
- Minced garlic — 1/2 tbsp

## Directions

1. Cut the kimchi into bite-size pieces and prepare the pork, tofu, and green onion.
2. Cook the pork in a pot over medium heat until the outside is no longer pink.
3. Add the kimchi and stir-fry for another 2 to 3 minutes, then pour in the water.
4. Add the gochugaru, soup soy sauce, and minced garlic. Simmer over medium-low heat for about 15 minutes.
5. Add the tofu and green onion, simmer for another 3 to 5 minutes, and adjust the seasoning to taste.
